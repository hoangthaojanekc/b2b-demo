Sales system & leadership's work

Source systems — HubSpot and Salesforce

HubSpot (or Marketo) captures every form fill, email click, and webinar registration; Salesforce holds the leads, accounts, and opportunities Sales works.

2

Extract — Fivetran or in-house

Raw records from both HubSpot and Salesforce are pulled into the warehouse on a schedule. A Python extract script works at demo scale; at real-world volume, a managed connector like Fivetran takes over the pagination, rate limits, and schema changes that get messy to maintain by hand.

The layer I own
3

Data warehouse — raw landing

Both platforms' data lands side by side, untouched: a single source of truth for what each system currently believes.

4
Identity resolution

Clean, match, assign ownership

Normalize company names, match records on email and domain, give every field one source of truth, log every exception.

5

Reverse ETL — feed back on change

An in-house connector replaces the platforms' native sync, which doesn't deduplicate contacts. Both systems' data comes down to the warehouse, gets cleaned and matched, and only then goes back out — HubSpot and Salesforce never sync directly.

HubSpot → warehouse → Salesforce

Clean, deduplicated contacts and their marketing data are pushed to Salesforce, matched to existing records so a re-sync updates instead of duplicating.

Salesforce → warehouse → HubSpot

Sales updates — opportunity stage, lead status, owning rep — flow back to HubSpot, so marketing sees what happened to every lead it sent.

If a native connector is still switched on, the warehouse feed overwrites it, so the clean version always wins. Every write, success or failure, lands in a sync log.

Where this usually breaks

Commercial connectors handle the extract well. These four gaps are what they routinely miss — and what step 4 above is built to catch.

Between steps 2 & 3

No filtering before sync

Every record moves across by default, whether it should reach the other system or not.

Inside step 4

Exact-match-only dedup

"Acme" and "Acme Inc." are treated as different companies, creating duplicate accounts.

Between steps 5 & 6

No stable external key

Re-syncing a record creates a new one instead of updating the existing one.

Throughout

No queryable error log

When a sync fails, most tools show a status, not a reason — nobody knows which records didn't make it across.
